Uni Navi — Interactive Mobile Prototype

Open index.html in a browser. The demo is completely static: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only.

Try these interactions:
1. Tap the search field and choose “T 1001”.
2. Press “Start navigation”.
3. Press “Simulate walking” several times to see outdoor-to-indoor transition.
4. Toggle layers such as Events, Entrances, Toilets, Defib, Study, Sockets, and Open now.
5. Tap map markers for cards such as Sommerfest, Building T entrance, WC, sockets, and opening information.

Notes:
- The campus map image is derived from the provided Universität Augsburg barrier-free map PDF.
- Opening times, availability, and position are mocked for prototype/demo purposes.
- Indoor floor plan is a schematic demo, not an official floor plan.
